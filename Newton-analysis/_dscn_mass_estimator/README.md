**2026-10-22** it holds only **made decisions**, on his ruling that VD-docs is
a space for decisions. A record is here because a decision is its subject: a
founding argument, a thesis, a proof, or a ruling. Content, provenance and
relations live in `catalog.yaml`; read the rows, not just the filenames.

## What may enter

A new record enters only if a decision is its subject. Work does not come here.
A new statement of the lemmas or of the equation is a new draft of its note,
not a record.
