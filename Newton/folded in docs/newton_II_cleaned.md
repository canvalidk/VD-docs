# Newton II — Cleaned Entries (Draft)

## Chimney (already drafted)

**E20 inertial acceleration** := "The acceleration of a point particle as
measured in an inertial frame."

> Non-law. References: acceleration (E8), inertial frame (E16).
> This is the bridge from Newton I to Newton II.

## Triplet

**E21 inertial acceleration** := "For a point particle, inertial acceleration
equals net force divided by inertial mass: a = F_net / m."

> **Role**: November (anchor). Previously defined by chimney (E20).
> The chimney says *what* inertial acceleration is (coordinate acceleration
> in an inertial frame); the triplet says *what determines it* (net force
> and inertial mass). The parenthetical "(in an inertial frame)" from v0.2
> is gone — that qualifier is now baked into the term itself.

**E22 net force** := "The vector quantity satisfying net force = inertial mass
· inertial acceleration for a point particle."

> **Role**: Winter 1. New term.
> Cleaned: "acceleration" → "inertial acceleration". Removed "(in an
> inertial frame)" — redundant now.

**E23 inertial mass** := "The positive scalar coefficient m such that
net force = m · inertial acceleration for a point particle."

> **Role**: Winter 2. New term.
> Same cleanup. The positivity constraint stays — it's part of the
> commitment Newton II makes.

## Notes on changes from v0.2

1. **"acceleration" → "inertial acceleration"** throughout. This is the main
   structural change. Newton II's November is no longer bare coordinate
   acceleration (E8) — it's the post-Newton-I concept that carries the
   inertial frame qualifier.

2. **"(in an inertial frame)" removed** from all three definitions. That
   phrase was doing work that the term "inertial acceleration" now handles
   by construction.

3. **Entry numbers shifted** to accommodate the expanded Newton I unit
   (E14–E19) and the inertial acceleration chimney (E20). Old E17–E19
   become E21–E23.
