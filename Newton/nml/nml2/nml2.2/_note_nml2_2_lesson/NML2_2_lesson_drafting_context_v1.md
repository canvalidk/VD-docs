## 4. Content specification

**(a) Trackability as ground.** Tracks from seeded scenes (B3.2); path vs
track (B3.1); tolerance bounded from below by tracker resolution and from
above by the question's budget (B3.4) — if no window exists, the model is not
supportable with the data.

**(b) Redundancy and lumping.** Formal two-track test (B3.5); all-pairs bundle
audits, cliques, non-transitivity, the human clique-pick (B3.6);
count-stability under densification — bodies are OUTPUTS (B3.7, O32);
representative-track licence and what it omits (B3.8); Galilean invariance of
the redundancy judgment, contrast with rotating frames (B3.8A).

**(c) The full persistence certificate — BOTH halves, taught explicitly.**
The computable redundancy audit AND the coverage probably-no: densification
audits (B3.3), sub-resolution drain (B3.25), the no-offshoot / reconstruction /
expiry taxonomy (B3.18). **Corrective requirement:** the v1 draft presented
the audit as if it were the whole licence — this lesson must NOT repeat that
error. Redundancy escorts a value; coverage limits the certificate; neither
alone is the licence.

**(e) Prediction vs retrospective repair** (B3.20; CQ14) and the

retaught — Newton II appears here as the CONSUMER whose licence this lesson
constructs (scene → tracks → redundancy under tolerance → bundle → target
address → mass address → validity history → licensed Newton-II use).
