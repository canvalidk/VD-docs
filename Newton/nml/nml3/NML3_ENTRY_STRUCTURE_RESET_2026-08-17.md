# NML3 Entry-Structure Reset — 2026-08-17

**Status:** reset record for the NML3 folder. Authoritative on *what is retired
and what survives*; not authoritative on the new entry structure, which is a
VD-Newton decision (see §5).
**Project:** VD Newton / NML3 — force-account construction
**Kind:** boundary marker. Not an excavated note, no `dN`, no successor line.

---

## 0. What happened

The NML3 work produced enough understanding of the force account — membership
warrant, closure, identity of impressed-force members, action/reaction
exclusivity — that the **entry structure itself is being re-cut**. The old cut
is no longer the target the downstream material was written for.

This is not a repair of the downstream material. The material was correct work
against a cut that is being withdrawn, so it is **retired as authority and kept
as reasoning record**, per the never-archive rule in `ORGANIZATION.md` and the
precedent set by the E10 packet line (`d2`–`d5`, each superseded as the working
account and each deliberately kept).

## 1. The split criterion

The reset is recorded **by layer, not by date**. The line is:

> Does this note's content depend on which entries exist and which wall each one
> carries?

- **`conceptual/`** — entry-cut-independent. These notes state the problem, the
  warrant gap, the consequences, the open identity problem, and the available
  vocabulary. A different cut does not disturb them. *They are the learning that
  caused the reset, and they are the input to the new cut.*
- **`entry-cut-dependent/`** — written against the retired cut. Read for
  reasoning, not for authority.

The advantage of the layer split over a dated generation folder: the same line
falls in the same place at the next reset, so the structure survives being used
twice.

## 4. What was *not* done, deliberately

- **No file was edited in place.** The retired notes carry no banner. The
  boundary lives in the folder placement, this record, and `catalog.yaml` — the

- **No new cut was invented here.** See §5.

## 5. Open: the new entry structure and the E-numbers

The new cut is **not recorded in this file**. Entry production is authoritative
in **VD-Newton**, and the E-numbers are shared across all of Newton (E8–E17 span
the entry passes and packets), so the numbering decision has to be made at source
and mirrored here.

The specific question left open:

> Does the new structure **reuse E13–E17** with different wall assignments, or
> **advance to fresh numbers**?

Reuse keeps continuity but makes every existing citation of "E16" ambiguous about
which structure it meant — including the citations inside
`entry-cut-dependent/NML3_ENTRY_TO_LESSON_BRIDGE_d1.md` and any VD-Newton pass
that references them. Fresh numbers cost continuity and keep citations honest.
Until this is settled at source, **treat bare E13–E17 references in this folder as
naming the retired cut.**

When the new cut lands, it goes in a new note at `nml3/` level (not into
`entry-cut-dependent/`, and not as a `dN` of the bridge — a re-cut is not a
successor draft of the thing it replaces), and this record gets a pointer to it.
